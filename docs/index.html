FAQ

Does DejaVu upload my data?

No. Everything stays on your PC.

Can I see who's in my match before it starts?

No. DejaVu only shows players once the match has started.

How much space does it use?

Less than 1 MB per 100 matches, even on the Full setting.

Can I back up my history?

Yes. Open your history with Alt+H and use Export as JSON. Import from JSON brings it back.

Can I change the hotkeys?

Yes. Open Overwolf's settings and go to Hotkeys.

The overlay says "Not receiving match data". What do I do?

Close Overwatch, leave Overwolf running, then launch Overwatch again. DejaVu picks up on its own once data arrives.

Never forget a player again

DejaVu remembers every player you meet, so you don't have to.
