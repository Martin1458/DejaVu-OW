FAQ

Does DejaVu upload my data?

No. Everything stays on your PC.

Can I see who's in my match before it starts?

No. DejaVu only shows players once the match has started.

How much space does it use?

Less than 1 MB per 100 matches, even on the Full setting.

Can I back up my history?

Yes. Open your history with Alt+H and use Export as JSON. Import from JSON brings it back.

Can I change the hotkeys?

Yes. Open Overwolf's settings and go to Hotkeys.

The overlay says "Not receiving match data". What do I do?

Close Overwatch, leave Overwolf running, then launch Overwatch again. DejaVu picks up on its own once data arrives.

Changelog

What changed in each build.

0.3.5 16 September 2026

  • The overlay stays fully on screen at lower game resolutions, and re-fits itself if you change resolution mid-game.
  • You can resize the history window while it is open over the game.
  • The welcome screen lets you choose whether the overlay can be dragged.
  • Dragging the corner badge works every time, and overlay text no longer gets selected by accident.

0.3.0 15 September 2026

  • The overlay hotkey moved from Alt+G to Alt+J, because NVIDIA's overlay claims Alt+G for itself.
  • The overlay now stays on screen: Alt+J switches between the full table and a small badge in the corner.
  • You can drag the overlay where you want it, and it remembers. Turn it on in Settings.
  • Teammate and enemy names are easier to tell apart at a glance.
  • Settings shows your hotkeys, always the ones actually bound.

0.2.0 14 September 2026

  • A welcome screen on first run for choosing what DejaVu remembers.
  • The overlay now says so when Overwolf's match data stops arriving, and how to fix it.
  • App icons, and the overlay window finally respects its own size.

0.1.0 7 September 2026

  • The first build: the in-game overlay, your searchable player history, notes and tags, leaderboards, three detail levels, and JSON export and import.
