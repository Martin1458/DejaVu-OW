Welcome to DejaVu

DejaVu remembers who you play with and against, and shows their history in an overlay during your match.

Welcome back

What DejaVu remembers

Everything stays on this PC. Nothing is uploaded. You can change or erase it at any time.

What the overlay shows

Greyed-out rows need a higher setting above. Your choice is saved either way.

Moving the overlay

While this is on, you can drag the overlay and its corner badge wherever you like, and they stay there. While it is off, clicks pass straight through to the game.

Changing this later

Known players ⚙
Press Alt+H to open or close your player history, then click the ⚙ for these same settings.
Press Alt+J during a match to hide or show the overlay.
